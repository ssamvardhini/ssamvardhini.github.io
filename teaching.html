Teaching

My teaching experience spans university courses, professional development programming, and K–12 science outreach, reflecting a commitment to scientific education across a range of audiences and levels of experience.

Graduate Student Instructor, MCELLBI 104 — University of California, Berkeley

I served as a Graduate Student Instructor for MCELLBI 104 across three semesters (Fall, Spring, and Fall), leading discussion sections and holding office hours to support students in developing a rigorous understanding of molecular and cell biology.

Python Boot Camp — Instructor, then Director

I began as an instructor and teaching assistant for the Python Boot Camp before later directing the program, through which incoming graduate students are introduced to the programming skills foundational to computational research.

Science Outreach — Bay Area Scientists in Schools (BASIS)

Beyond the university classroom, I have volunteered with BASIS, a program connecting scientists with K–12 classrooms to foster early engagement with science, including a presentation to a third-grade classroom.

Across these roles, my teaching has consistently received strong evaluations, which are available upon request.
